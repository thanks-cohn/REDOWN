# Seedr — REDOWN competitor brief

**Category:** hosted remote-fetch and cloud-storage service  
**Priority:** P2

## What the competitor does well
Cloud-hosted fetching rather than forcing local device to stay active; easy browser-accessible downloads.

## What we should borrow as product principles
Queue remote jobs with durable lifecycle, convenient add-link entry points, simple ready state.

## Where REDOWN could win
Customer-owned destination clouds and provisioned cloud workers instead of tying users to one hosted locker; auditable delivery to many destinations.

## Where they can beat us / reality check
Real remote-execution competition; hosted simplicity may beat BYO-infrastructure onboarding.

## REDOWN API and cold-agent implications
Source URL classification (direct/protected), preflight accessibility, staging states, explicit remote-fetch engine selection.

## Concrete competitive / QA experiment
