# Save to Google Drive (Google) — REDOWN competitor brief

**Category:** official single-cloud browser extension  
**Priority:** P0  
**Status:** Strategy with cited starting points; manually verify product versions and feature availability before implementing claims.

## What the competitor does well
Simple right-click save, save webpage content, and advertised browser screenshot-to-Google-Drive support; huge existing adoption.

## What we should borrow as product principles
Single-provider low-friction save and discoverable actions, constrained settings, fast first success.

## Where REDOWN could win
One capture to chosen cloud set, R2/GCS/MEGA S4 and other supported destinations, inventory/verification and human-readable statuses.

## Where they can beat us / reality check
High as free familiar default. Crucial correction: screenshot-to-cloud is NOT novel in itself.

## REDOWN API and cold-agent implications
Capture artifact provider-agnostic schema; /destinations group CRUD; per-target permission checks and verified receipts.

## Concrete competitive / QA experiment
Save web page/screenshot in official extension vs REDOWN to Google; then compare a multi-provider task.

## Rules of engagement
Maintain REDOWN's one-click DONE workflow, user-owned execution model where authorized, no false transfer or replica success, no silent destructive cleanup, auditable provider scopes and error handling, accessible simple mode, desktop expansion via shared experience. Competitor strengths are inspiration; do not copy proprietary assets or obscure needed confirmation.
