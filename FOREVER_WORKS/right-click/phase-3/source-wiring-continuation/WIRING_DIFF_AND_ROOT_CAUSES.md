## Discriminating next observation

At the matching installed build, privately compare one synthetic failing intent against: draft stable ID/path; saved menu projection/signature/version; active mode; M.menuEntries output; callback chronology; actual native item/context; fresh C.resolve result; permitted source selection; provider receipt. Stop at first divergent boundary. No provider writes, prepare handlers or customer-state rewrite are needed to diagnose pre-transfer gaps. A save/native receipt without rendering is insufficient; a native click without exact provider receipt/bytes is insufficient.

Later coding can improve view ownership, readiness visibility, naming and feedback. It must preserve single guarded persistence, deep hierarchy, fresh resolver, empty-model dominance, visible menu-only red × and safe transfer behavior. That is a separate task and is not executed here.
